■ STRATEGIC BRIEFING — EYES ONLY

This is a turn command simulation. On each round, select ONE ORDER, review its effects, and commit it. The adversary then receives exactly one turn.

Rounds 1–3 assess intent. Rounds 4–7 test posture and diplomacy. From round 8, you may pursue a mutual stand-down or initiate a dual-control release ceremony.

Nuclear controls remain physically locked until the commander reads back a one-time authenticator, National Command Authority matches it, and the President grants release authority.

After authorization, combat also alternates: one strike package, resolution, then one enemy package. ABM interception remains a defensive reaction.
